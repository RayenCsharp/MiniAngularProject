About the site

Souk El Khorda is an online second-hand shop. Every item here has been used before and is waiting for a new owner. Browse the items, check their condition and price, and send an offer if you find something you like.

How it works

1

Browse

Look through the items and search by name or price.

2

Choose

Open an item to see its photo, condition, and details.

3

Make an offer

Propose your price. If it is accepted, the seller contacts you.
